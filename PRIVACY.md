# Blanket Remote privacy policy

_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

Blanket Remote lets you control your own computer from your own phone over your home Wi-Fi.

**What it collects:** nothing. Blanket Remote has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of its own.

**What stays on your devices:**
- The PC app and the phone talk to each other directly on your local network. Touchpad moves, clicks, typing
  and key presses go from your phone to your PC and nowhere else.
- To pair, the PC app shows a QR code with a private key. The key and the names of your PC and phone are saved
  on your own devices so they reconnect by themselves. Making a new key on the PC disconnects every phone.
- The PC app keeps a small log file on your PC for troubleshooting. It is never sent anywhere.

**Internet use:** the app only opens a website (YouTube, Netflix, ARY+, Gmail or WhatsApp Web) in your PC's
browser when you tap it in the Apps tab. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

**Children:** Blanket Remote does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be sent through the
[Blanket Remote GitHub page](https://github.com/Keliph/Remotely/issues).
